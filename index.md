# Cocalicious Privacy Policy

_Effective: October 18, 2026_

Cocalicious helps you find restaurants that serve Coke or Pepsi. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

## Information we collect

**Location.** With your permission, the app uses your device's location to show restaurants near you. Your location is sent to our server only to look up nearby places and is not saved to your account or stored by us. You can turn off location access in your device settings and still browse the map.

**An anonymous account.** The first time you save, answer or suggest something, the app creates an anonymous account identified by a random ID. It is not linked to your name, phone number or contacts.

**Email address (optional).** If you sign in with email, we store your email address to send you a sign-in code and to keep your account across devices. We do not send marketing email.

**What you contribute.** We store what you add to the app with your account ID:
- answers about whether a place serves Coke or Pepsi
- star ratings and reviews, and reports of reviews
- saved (favorite) places
- restaurants you add or suggest, and corrections you send
- your display name, if you set one

Reviews and display names are shown to other users. Answers and suggestions are combined with other users' to show what a place serves; they are not shown with your name.

## How we use information

Only to run the app: show nearby places, combine answers, display reviews, keep you signed in, and prevent abuse (for example, limiting how many suggestions one account can send per hour). We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

## Services we use

- **Supabase** stores the app's data and handles sign-in.
- **Google Maps** displays the map. When you use the map or add a restaurant, Google receives the map area or the location of the place you tapped. Google's use of that information is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **OpenStreetMap** supplies the restaurant list. No information about you is sent to OpenStreetMap.

## Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your contributions while your account exists. To delete your account and everything linked to it, email us at [colacudas@gmail.com](mailto:colacudas@gmail.com) from the address you signed in with, and we will delete it within 30 days. If you never signed in, your anonymous account is not linked to any personal details; contact us and we will help remove what you can identify, such as your reviews. Restaurants you added stay in the app, without any link to you.

## Children

Cocalicious is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
